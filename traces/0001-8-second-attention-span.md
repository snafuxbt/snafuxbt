# 0001: The 8-second attention span

**Verdict:** no measurement found. The figure goes back to a stats website with vague sources, then a Microsoft Canada report that quoted it, then everywhere.

## Chain
1. **Microsoft Canada, "Attention spans", Spring 2015.** A real survey (2,000 Canadians, 112 on EEG). The 12s / 8s / goldfish 9s figures appear on p.6 with the footnote "Source: Statistic brain". Microsoft's own method splits people into thirds by game scores and never measures attention in seconds. [PDF](https://storage.googleapis.com/public.papers.www.brainsights.com/Microsoft_Attention%20Spans.pdf)
2. **Statistic Brain, archived 2015-05-04.** Lists 8.25s (2015), 12s (2000), goldfish 9s. Source given: "National Center for Biotechnology Information, U.S. National Library of Medicine, The Associated Press", with no paper, author or year. [Archive](http://web.archive.org/web/20150504135207/http://www.statisticbrain.com/attention-span-statistics/)
3. **BBC More or Less, 2017.** Asked the listed sources; neither NCBI nor the AP could find research behind the stats. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/health-38896790)
